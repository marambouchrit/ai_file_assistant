1. Release 3.4 status

Tom reported that release 3.4 of the mobile app is feature complete. Two blocking bugs remain: the
offline sync loses edits when the device changes time zone, and the export to CSV produces an empty
file for accounts with more than 10,000 records. Both fixes are in review. The release date moves
from 20 May to 27 May to leave time for a full regression run.

Sofia asked whether the dark mode setting could still be included. The team agreed to postpone it to
release 3.5 because the design review is not finished.

2. Customer feedback

Jonas presented the support numbers for April: 1,240 tickets were opened, 18% fewer than in March.
The median first response time was 3 hours 10 minutes. The top three topics were password resets,
billing questions and the new calendar view. Several customers complained that the calendar view
does not show public holidays.

Leila will prepare two design options for holiday display and share them by 22 May.

3. Pricing experiment

The experiment with the annual plan discount ran for four weeks on 5,000 trial accounts. The 20%
discount increased annual plan conversion from 6.1% to 8.4%, while the 30% discount reached 8.9%.
Because the extra gain from 30% is small, the team decided to keep the 20% discount and roll it out
to all new trials on 2 June.

4. Hiring

candidates are in the final round for the backend role. The QA position has been open for nine
weeks with few qualified applicants; Tom will ask recruiting to widen the search to remote
candidates in neighbouring countries.
